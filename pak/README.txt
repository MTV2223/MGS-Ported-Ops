The locked packs go in this folder.

launcher_assets.pak  - the launcher's FOXHOUND intro and logo (comes with the port; opens only with your own disc)
MGSPO-HD-*.pak       - the HD packs (upscaled movies and textures): download them from the Releases page,
                       put them here, then choose DISC CHECK > INSTALL HD PACKS in the launcher.
                       After installing you can delete them.
